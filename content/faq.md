What equipment do I need for home yoga?

Just a comfortable floor space and a standard yoga mat. Our instructors bring any specialized props required for your personalized session.

How flexible are session timings?

We offer flexible rescheduling options tailored for busy corporate and family schedules. Our team works with you to find a consistent time that fits your week.

Are sessions suitable for beginners or specific needs?

Yes, our safety-first sessions are tailored for all levels, including beginners, seniors, and those seeking posture recovery or mobility support.

Which Bangalore neighborhoods do you serve?

We deliver personalized home yoga across premier Bangalore neighborhoods like Indiranagar, HSR Layout, Whitefield, Koramangala, and Sarjapur Road.

Can I try a session before committing?

Yes, we offer an initial consultation to discuss your needs and ensure a perfect match with our instructors before you begin your regular practice.
