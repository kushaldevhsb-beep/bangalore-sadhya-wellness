## 3. Typography System

The website combines classical serif elegance with clean, highly readable modern sans-serif fonts:

1. **Serif Display / Headings**:
   - **Font**: `Hedvig Letters Serif`, serif (Google Fonts)
   - **Weights**: Regular (400)
   - **Usage**: Hero H1 titles, section H2 headlines, testimonial quotes. Provides a calm, holistic, academic authority.

2. **Primary Sans / Body**:
   - **Font**: `Manrope`, sans-serif (Google Fonts)
   - **Weights**: Regular (400), Medium (500), SemiBold (600)
   - **Usage**: Paragraphs, service descriptions, navigation items, buttons.

3. **Secondary / Utility**:
   - **Font**: `Roboto`, sans-serif (Google Fonts)
   - **Weights**: Regular (400), Medium (500)
   - **Usage**: Numbers, step badges (`01`, `02`), stats counters (`15+ Yrs`), form inputs.

### Typography Hierarchy Scale
- **H1 (Hero)**: `40px - 56px` | Line-height: `1.15` | Hedvig Letters Serif
- **H2 (Section Titles)**: `32px - 40px` | Line-height: `1.25` | Hedvig Letters Serif
- **H3 (Cards / Services)**: `22px - 26px` | Line-height: `1.3` | Hedvig Letters Serif / Manrope Bold
- **Body Large**: `18px - 20px` | Line-height: `1.5` | Manrope
- **Body Standard**: `15px - 16px` | Line-height: `1.6` | Manrope
- **Caption / Meta**: `13px - 14px` | Line-height: `1.4` | Roboto / Manrope
